A BrightBeginningStarts Here

Welcome to Kids-R-Kreative Learning Center, a trusted childcare provider in Mangonia Park and West Palm Beach, FL. For over 20 years, we've offered a safe, nurturing environment where children grow socially, emotionally, and academically through infant care, child care, after-school programs, and Voluntary Prekindergarten (VPK) for children ages 6 weeks to 12 years.

Our experienced teachers use the Frog Street approach to build a strong foundation for every child's next steps in learning. Schedule a tour today and see why families across Mangonia Park, West Palm Beach, and Riviera Beach trust us with their children.
