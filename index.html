About Us

Welcome to Kids-R-Kreative Learning Center

Welcome to Kids-R-Kreative Learning Center, a trusted daycare and VPK provider in Mangonia Park and West Palm Beach, FL. For over 20 years, we've offered a safe, nurturing environment where children grow socially, emotionally, and academically through infant care, child care, after-school programs, and Voluntary Prekindergarten (VPK) for children ages 6 weeks to 12 years.

Our experienced teachers use the Frog Street approach to build a strong foundation for every child's next steps in learning. Schedule a tour today and see why families across Mangonia Park, West Palm Beach, and Riviera Beach trust us with their children.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from parents in Mangonia Park & West Palm Beach.

What ages does Kids-R-Kreative serve?

We care for children ages 6 weeks to 12 years, offering infant care, child care, VPK, and after-school programs in Mangonia Park, FL.

Is Kids-R-Kreative a licensed child care provider?

Yes, we are a Florida-licensed child care provider serving families in Mangonia Park, West Palm Beach, and Riviera Beach since 2006.

Do you offer VPK (Voluntary Prekindergarten) in West Palm Beach?

Yes! We are a VPK provider offering school-readiness programs for children ages 4–5 using the Frog Street curriculum.

What are your hours of operation?

We are open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM at 4480 S Tiffany Dr, Mangonia Park, FL 33407.

Do you provide transportation for after-school care?

Yes, transportation is available for our after-school program. Contact us at 561-296-2462 for details.
