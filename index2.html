About Us

Welcome to Kids-R-Kreative Learning Center

For over 20 years, we've offered a safe, nurturing environment where children grow socially, emotionally, and academically through infant care, child care, after-school programs, and Voluntary Prekindergarten (VPK) for children ages 6 weeks to 12 years.

Our experienced teachers use the Frog Street approach to build a strong foundation for every child's next steps in learning.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages does Kids-R-Kreative serve?

We care for children ages 6 weeks to 12 years, offering infant care, child care, VPK, and after-school programs in Mangonia Park, FL.

Is Kids-R-Kreative a licensed child care provider?

Yes, we are a Florida-licensed child care provider serving families in Mangonia Park, West Palm Beach, and Riviera Beach since 2006.

Do you offer VPK in West Palm Beach?

Yes! We are a VPK provider offering school-readiness programs for children ages 4–5 using the Frog Street curriculum.

What are your hours of operation?

We are open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM at 4480 S Tiffany Dr, Mangonia Park, FL 33407.

Do you provide transportation for after-school care?

Yes, transportation is available for our after-school program. Contact us at 561-296-2462 for details.
